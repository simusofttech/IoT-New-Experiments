Aim

To create a smart two-bay parking indicator using an ESP32 and ultrasonic sensors that detects whether each parking bay is available or occupied. The system uses green and red LEDs for visual indication and displays the parking status on an OLED display.

Components Required

ESP32 DevKitC (×1)

Main controller of the parking system.

HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor (×2)

Measures the distance to detect a vehicle in each parking bay.

Green LED (×2)

Indicates that a parking bay is available.

Red LED (×2)

Indicates that a parking bay is occupied.

220Ω Resistor (×4)

Limits the current flowing through the LEDs.

0.96" SSD1306 OLED (×1)

Displays parking bay status, distance and free slots.

Breadboard (×1)

Provides an easy base for making the circuit connections.

Jumper Wires

Used to connect the ESP32, sensors, LEDs and OLED.

Parking Bay Indication

🟢 AVAILABLE

When no vehicle is detected within 10 cm, the green LED turns ON.

🔴 OCCUPIED

When a vehicle is detected within 10 cm, the red LED turns ON.

How Does It Work?

1

Measure Bay 1

The first HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor measures the distance between the sensor and the object in Bay 1.

2

Measure Bay 2

The second ultrasonic sensor performs the same measurement for Bay 2.

3

Check the Distance

The ESP32 compares each measured distance with the 10 cm parking detection distance.

4

Show Available Bay

If the distance is greater than or equal to 10 cm, the bay is considered available and its green LED turns ON.

5

Show Occupied Bay

If the measured distance is less than 10 cm, the bay is considered occupied and its red LED turns ON.

6

Update the OLED

The OLED shows the status and measured distance of both bays along with the number of free parking spaces.

Procedure

  1. Place the ESP32 on the breadboard.
  2. Connect two HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors to the ESP32.
  3. Connect the green and red LEDs through 220Ω resistors.
  4. Connect the SSD1306 OLED display.
  5. Check all VCC and GND connections.
  6. Connect the ESP32 to your computer.
  7. Upload the program to the ESP32.
  8. Open the Serial Monitor at 115200 baud.
  9. Place an object in front of Ultrasonic Sensor 1.
  10. Observe the Bay 1 red LED turn ON when the object is closer than 10 cm.
  11. Remove the object and observe the green LED turn ON.
  12. Repeat the test with Bay 2.
  13. Observe the parking status and free-space count on the OLED display.

Result

The ESP32 successfully monitors two parking bays using ultrasonic sensors. A green LED indicates an available bay, while a red LED indicates an occupied bay. The OLED displays the condition of both parking bays, their measured distances and the total number of free spaces.
