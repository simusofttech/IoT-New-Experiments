Aim

To develop a smart water tank level monitoring system using an ESP32 development board and HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor, and to display the water level on a local Wi-Fi dashboard.

The experiment also demonstrates Telegram notification and buzzer alerts when the water level becomes nearly empty or full.

About the Experiment

The Smart Water Tank Level Monitoring System measures the distance between an ultrasonic sensor mounted at the top of a tank and the water surface.

The ESP32 calculates the approximate filling percentage based on a configured reference distance. The result is classified into EMPTY, NORMAL and FULL states.

A local Wi-Fi dashboard displays the estimated water percentage, measured distance, tank visualization and current water-level status.

On entering the EMPTY or FULL state, the system activates a buzzer briefly and attempts to send a Telegram notification to the configured recipient.

The system provides monitoring and notification only. It does not control a pump or automatically fill or drain the water tank.

Electrical Safety:

Do not directly connect the 5V HC-SR04 ECHO output to ESP32 GPIO18.

Use a properly rated logic-level shifter, maintain common ground, and check the active buzzer's voltage and current requirements.

GPIO5 also has boot-strapping considerations on the ESP32. Verify reliable startup with the connected sensor.

Keep the ultrasonic module away from splashes and condensation. Standard HC-SR04 modules are not waterproof.

Ultrasonic Distance Measurement

The ESP32 sends a short trigger pulse to the HC-SR04. The sensor returns an ECHO pulse whose duration relates to the measured distance.

Distance Calculation

Distance is estimated by multiplying echo duration by the approximate speed of sound and dividing by two for the outgoing and returning ultrasonic path.

Water Percentage

The program subtracts measured distance from the configured reference tank height, then converts the result to a percentage.

Status Classification

The percentage is classified as EMPTY at 10% or below, FULL at 90% or above, and NORMAL between those thresholds.

Telegram Alert

When the state changes to EMPTY or FULL, the ESP32 attempts to send an appropriate Telegram message using the configured bot credentials.

Buzzer Alert

The active buzzer is activated briefly on entry into an alert condition.

Dashboard Visualization

The ESP32 serves a web dashboard showing estimated filling percentage, distance, a graphical water-level indicator and current tank status.

Procedure

  1. Arrange the ESP32, HC-SR04, buzzer, breadboard, jumper wires and suitable ECHO logic-level shifter.
  2. Mount the ultrasonic sensor above the water surface in a splash-protected location.
  3. Connect HC-SR04 VCC to its compatible 5V supply.
  4. Connect HC-SR04 GND to common GND.
  5. Connect HC-SR04 TRIG to GPIO5.
  6. Connect HC-SR04 ECHO to GPIO18 through the level shifter.
  7. Connect the active buzzer signal to GPIO19 using a driver if required.
  8. Connect buzzer GND to common ground.
  9. Verify the wiring and voltage levels before powering the ESP32.
  10. Install the ESP32 board package in Arduino IDE.
  11. Copy the complete sketch from Explore the Code.
  12. Enter your Wi-Fi credentials in the sketch.
  13. Configure the Telegram bot token and recipient chat ID privately.
  14. Replace the example 100 cm tank reference height with the measured value.
  15. Verify and upload the Arduino sketch.
  16. Open Serial Monitor at 115200 baud.
  17. Note the ESP32 dashboard IP address.
  18. Open the IP address in a browser on the same Wi-Fi network.
  19. Observe the water percentage, distance and tank visualization.
  20. Safely test EMPTY, NORMAL and FULL conditions.
  21. Verify the buzzer and Telegram notification attempts at the EMPTY and FULL boundaries.
