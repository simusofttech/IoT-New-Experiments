Aim

To control three LEDs using an Arduino UNO R3, a VS1838B IR receiver and an IR remote.

About the Experiment

The Arduino receives commands from the IR remote through the 1838B IR receiver connected to digital pin D2.

Different remote buttons select the Green, Red and Yellow LEDs or switch all three LEDs ON or OFF. The received command and current status are also shown in the Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.

Procedure

  1. Assemble the IR receiver, three LEDs, three 220Ω resistors and jumper wires on the breadboard.
  2. Connect the IR receiver VCC to 5V, GND to GND and OUT to D2.
  3. Connect the Green LED through 220Ω to D8, Red LED through 220Ω to D9 and Yellow LED through 220Ω to D10.
  4. Connect all LED cathodes to common GND.
  5. Open Arduino IDE and select Arduino Uno.
  6. Install the IRremote library.
  7. Create Digital_IR_Remote_LED_Controller.ino and upload the program.
  8. Open Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
  9. Point the IR remote toward the receiver and test Buttons 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6.
Important: The SOP uses 0x1F for CMD_YELLOW as a value to replace if necessary. Press Button 6, read its actual command in Serial Monitor and update the value if your remote is different.
