Aim

To detect vibration using a digital vibration sensor module and automatically switch an LED ON using an Arduino UNO R3.

About the Experiment

The vibration sensor module provides a digital output to Arduino pin D2. The Arduino continuously checks this signal.

When vibration is detected, the LED connected to D8 through a 220Ω resistor turns ON and the Serial Monitor displays VIBRATION DETECTED. When the sensor is still, the LED remains OFF.

Procedure

  1. Place the Arduino UNO, vibration sensor, LED and resistor on the breadboard setup.
  2. Connect vibration sensor VCC to 5V and GND to Arduino GND.
  3. Connect vibration sensor DO to digital pin D2.
  4. Connect the LED anode to D8 through a 220Ω resistor.
  5. Connect the LED cathode to GND.
  6. Open Arduino IDE and select Arduino Uno.
  7. Save the sketch as Vibration_Activated_Lamp.ino and upload it to the Arduino.
  8. Open Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
  9. Keep the sensor still and verify that the LED remains OFF.
  10. Tap or gently shake the vibration sensor and verify that the LED turns ON.
Sensor polarity note: The supplied program treats HIGH as vibration detected. Some vibration modules may use the opposite digital-output polarity. If required, change HIGH to LOW in the detection condition.

Explore more: vibration sensor logic

The sensor module converts vibration into a digital HIGH or LOW signal. Its onboard sensitivity control can be adjusted so that small vibrations do not trigger the system unnecessarily.

Expected Output

With no vibration, the LED remains OFF. When vibration is detected, the LED turns ON and the Serial Monitor displays STATUS : VIBRATION DETECTED and LED : ON.

Result

The Vibration Activated Lamp successfully detects vibration through the digital sensor module and switches the LED ON whenever vibration is detected.
