Aim

To combine gas, vibration, temperature and humidity monitoring into one Arduino-based industrial warning panel.

About the Experiment

The Arduino reads MQ-2 gas level on A0, vibration on D2, and DHT11 temperature/humidity on D4.

During normal conditions the green LED remains ON. Gas, vibration, high temperature, or a DHT11 error activates the red LED and buzzer while the 16×2 I2C LCD shows the warning.

Procedure

  1. Connect MQ-2 AO to A0, VCC to 5V and GND to GND.
  2. Connect vibration sensor DO to D2.
  3. Connect DHT11 DATA to D4. A bare 4-pin DHT11 requires a 10kΩ pull-up between VCC and DATA.
  4. Connect I2C LCD SDA to A4 and SCL to A5.
  5. Connect buzzer to D8, red LED to D9 and green LED to D10 through 220Ω resistors.
  6. Install DHT sensor library, Adafruit Unified Sensor and LiquidCrystal I2C.
  7. Upload the program and open Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
  8. Allow the MQ-2 to warm up, then test vibration and safe controlled threshold conditions.
