Aim

To design and implement a rain detection system using an Arduino UNO R3 and a rain sensor module, which provides visual and audible alerts through an LED and buzzer whenever rain or water is detected.

About the Experiment

The Rain Alert Indicator is a simple automated monitoring system that detects the presence of water using a rain sensor module.

The sensor consists of a sensing plate and a comparator module. When water comes into contact with the sensing plate, the electrical resistance between its conductive tracks changes.

The comparator module converts this change into a digital signal, which is read by the Arduino UNO. In this experiment, a LOW signal indicates rain detection, while a HIGH signal indicates dry conditions.

When rain is detected, the Arduino activates a red LED and an active buzzer. When the sensor becomes dry, both indicators are switched OFF.

Important: The detection threshold can be adjusted using the potentiometer on the rain sensor module. The program assumes an active-LOW digital output.

Procedure

  1. Place the Arduino UNO, breadboard, rain sensor module, LED, resistor and buzzer on the working surface.
  2. Connect the rain sensor module VCC pin to Arduino 5V.
  3. Connect the rain sensor GND pin to Arduino GND.
  4. Connect the rain sensor DO pin to Arduino digital pin 2.
  5. Connect the red LED anode to digital pin 8 through a 220Ω resistor.
  6. Connect the LED cathode to GND.
  7. Connect the active buzzer positive terminal to digital pin 9 and its negative terminal to GND.
  8. Open the Arduino IDE and select Arduino UNO as the board.
  9. Enter the Arduino program given below.
  10. Verify and upload the program.
  11. Open the Serial Monitor and set the baud rate to 9600.
  12. Keep the rain sensor plate dry and observe that the LED and buzzer remain OFF.
  13. Apply a small amount of water to the sensor plate and observe the LED and buzzer turning ON.
  14. Dry the sensing plate and observe the system returning to its normal state.
