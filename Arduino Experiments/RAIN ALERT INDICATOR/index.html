Aim

To detect rain or water using a rain sensor module and provide visual and audible alerts using an LED and buzzer.

About the Experiment

The Arduino UNO R3 reads the rain sensor digital output on D2.

For the supplied SOP program, LOW means rain/water detected. When rain is detected, the LED on D8 and buzzer on D9 turn ON; otherwise both remain OFF.

Procedure

  1. Connect the rain sensor VCC to 5V, GND to GND and DO to D2.
  2. Connect the LED anode to D8 through a 220Ω resistor and cathode to GND.
  3. Connect buzzer positive to D9 and negative to GND.
  4. Upload the Arduino program and open Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
  5. Keep the sensor dry and verify NO RAIN.
  6. Place a small amount of water on the sensing plate and verify RAIN DETECTED.
